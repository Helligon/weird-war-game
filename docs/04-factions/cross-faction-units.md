# Cross-faction units

## Summary
Sometimes a Faction gains Sections from outside its own roster: Sections lent by allies, defectors, or enemies turned by dark magic.

## Rules
- **XFU-001** (`decided`): The Entente Factions (Eastern and Western Allies) MAY occasionally receive a **seconded** Section from the other Entente Faction.
- **XFU-002** (`decided`): A Faction MAY gain a **defected** Section from the opposing side (e.g. a Defected Witch Section).
- **XFU-003** (`decided`): **Thrall / Ghoul** Sections (infantry turned by a vampire or other dark magic) MUST exist. They MAY be their own distinct unit type rather than a copy of an enemy unit.
- **XFU-004** (`proposed`): Asymmetry: the Entente gains extra Sections through **cooperation** (seconded), while the Central Powers gain them by **turning enemies**. After a victory, routed enemy infantry MAY be converted into Thralls through a Card or an officer ability.
- **XFU-005** (`proposed`): Defectors SHOULD come from Cards or random events.
