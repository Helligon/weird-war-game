# Factions overview

## Summary
Three playable Factions. Each one has the [[standard-roster]] plus faction-specific Sections that reflect its flavour.

| Faction | Nations | Flavour |
|---|---|---|
| [[central-powers]] | Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire, Bulgaria | Occult and undead (theme proposed) |
| [[eastern-allies]] | Russia, Japan | Beasts and spirits (theme proposed) |
| [[western-allies]] | Britain, France | Science and machines (theme proposed) |

## Rules
- **FAC-001** (`decided`): There MUST be three Factions: Central Powers, Eastern Allies, Western Allies.
- **FAC-002** (`decided`): Faction names MAY change (working names).
- **FAC-003** (`decided`): Every Faction MUST have access to the [[standard-roster]].
- **FAC-004** (`decided`): Every Faction MUST have faction-specific Sections that reflect its flavour.
- **FAC-005** (`decided`): The Central Powers MUST have more unit variety than the other Factions, since they are the only non-Entente option.
- **FAC-006** (`proposed`): To balance FAC-005, the Eastern and Western Allies SHOULD get **depth** instead of variety: fewer special units with stronger synergies.
- **FAC-008** (`decided`): Nations MUST NOT have nation-wide bonuses for now. Nations get their flavour through their units.
- **FAC-009** (`proposed` by Alex, tentative): Sections MAY carry nation or Faction tags with small modifiers (e.g. German Sections cheaper to supply, British Sections better morale).
- **FAC-007** (`decided`): The Eastern and Western Allies are both Entente.

## Open questions
- Q-FAC-01: Final Faction names.
- Q-FAC-02 (answered → FAC-008, FAC-009): No nation-wide bonuses; Sections may carry nation tags with modifiers.
