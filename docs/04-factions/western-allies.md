# Western Allies

## Summary
Britain and France. Proposed theme: **science and machines**.

## Rules
- **WAL-001** (`decided`): Nations: Britain, France.
- **WAL-002** (`decided`): Britain MUST have science-fiction / steampunk mechanised Sections.
- **WAL-003** (`proposed`): French Sections: radium or lightning weapons, or a Foreign Legion with experimental technology.
- **WAL-004** (`decided`): MAY occasionally receive seconded Sections from the Eastern Allies (see [[cross-faction-units]]).
