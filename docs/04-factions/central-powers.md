# Central Powers

## Summary
Germany, Austria-Hungary, the Ottoman Empire and Bulgaria. Proposed theme: **occult and undead**. Has the widest unit variety.

## Rules
- **CPW-001** (`decided`): Nations: Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire (Türkiye), Bulgaria.
- **CPW-002** (`decided`): Germany MUST have **Occultists**.
- **CPW-003** (`decided`): Austria-Hungary MUST have **Vampires**.
- **CPW-004** (`decided`): The Central Powers MUST have more unit variety than other Factions (FAC-005).
- **CPW-005** (`proposed`): Ottoman Sections: djinn, sand golems.
- **CPW-006** (`proposed`): Bulgarian Sections: zmey (dragon-like creatures from Bulgarian folklore), witch-herbalists.
- **CPW-007** (`proposed`): The Central Powers SHOULD get extra Sections by **turning enemies** (Thralls/Ghouls). See [[cross-faction-units]].
