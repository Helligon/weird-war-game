# Standard roster

## Summary
Every Faction has access to a standard set of Section types. Faction-specific Sections are added on top (see the faction notes).

## Rules
- **ROS-001** (`decided`): Every Faction MUST have access to the standard roster below.
- **ROS-002** (`decided`): The standard roster MAY be extended ("possibly some others").

| Section | Role | Class | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riflemen | All-rounder | Infantry | decided |
| Shotgunners | Close range | Infantry | decided |
| Snipers | Long range | Infantry | decided |
| Sappers | Anti-armour | Support | decided (class proposed) |
| Heavy infantry | Mechanised armoured infantry | Heavy | decided |
| Medic | Healing / damage reduction | Support | decided |
| Machine gunners | Suppression | Infantry | proposed |
| Flamethrowers | Area denial, trench clearing | Infantry | proposed |
| Mortar team | Light artillery | Artillery | proposed |
| Field gun | Basic artillery | Artillery | proposed |
| Light armoured car | Basic vehicle | Heavy | proposed |

### Medic
- **ROS-003** (`decided`): Medics MUST reduce damage taken and/or heal Units during battle.
- **ROS-004** (`decided`): Medic effects MUST be passive or automatic (no player-triggered abilities; see [[commands#Abilities]]).
- **ROS-005** (`proposed`): A Medic SHOULD reduce desertion losses for its Brigade.

### Other role notes (proposed)
- **ROS-006** (`proposed`): Snipers MAY target officers to break morale.
- **ROS-007** (`proposed`): Sappers MAY lay mines and wire automatically.

## Parameters
- Stats per Section type (health, Units per Section, damage, range, speed, SP cost) are **open** and will be defined in a later balance pass.
