# Section veterancy

## Summary
Sections that survive battles gain small random bonuses.

## Rules
- **VET-001** (`decided`): A Section that survives a battle MUST be eligible to gain veterancy.
- **VET-002** (`decided`): Veterancy MUST grant small random bonuses, e.g. higher morale, more HP, higher accuracy.
- **VET-003** (`decided`): Veterancy MUST belong to the Section, not the Brigade.
- **VET-004** (`proposed`): Veterancy SHOULD have ranks (P-VET-01). Each rank adds one random trait from a class-appropriate pool (Snipers get accuracy and range; Heavy gets HP and armour).
- **VET-005** (`proposed`): Healing a Section from very low health SHOULD risk losing a trait, as replacement recruits dilute the veterans.
- **VET-008** (`decided`): Surviving Sections on **both** sides MUST have a chance to gain veterancy. The winning side MUST have a higher chance (P-VET-04).
- **VET-009** (`decided`): Auto-resolved battles MUST also grant veterancy, at a lower chance than played battles.
- ~~VET-006~~: withdrawn (ARO-013).
- **VET-007** (`decided`): Section veterancy MUST travel with the Section between Brigades, and is subject to the +20% veterancy cap (OVT-007).
