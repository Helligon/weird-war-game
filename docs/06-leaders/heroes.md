# Heroes

## Summary
Heroes are named characters who fight with special weapons and gear. A Brigade can have at most one. The player can play as a hero in battle. Heroes are gained through Cards, campaign story events or achievements, and they grow through veterancy like officers.

## Leader types
| Type | How you get them | Bonuses / power | Progression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generic Officer | Always available | None | – |
| ARO → Officer | Promote a Unit, 5 battles | Earned (3 slots) | ARO system, then officer veterancy |
| Officer (named) | Collection, achievements | Preset, flavourful (3 slots) | Officer veterancy |
| Hero | Cards, story events, achievements | Special weapons and gear | Veterancy like officers |

## Rules
- **HER-001** (`decided`): Heroes MUST be named characters.
- **HER-002** (`decided`): The player MAY play as a hero in battle (see [[player-character]]).
- **HER-003** (`decided`): Heroes MUST be subject to permadeath under Iron Officers (OFF-009), and wounded in standard mode (OFF-010).
- **HER-008** (`decided`): There MUST be no separate "Captain" tier. Named leaders from the collection are Officers.
- **HER-009** (`decided`): A Brigade MUST have at most one hero (P-HER-01).
- **HER-010** (`decided`): Heroes MUST be distinguished by **special weapons and gear** (e.g. armour, a shield).
- **HER-011** (`deferred`): Heroes MAY gain active abilities at a later stage.
- **HER-012** (`decided`): Heroes MUST be obtainable through CP Cards, campaign story events, **and** achievement unlocks (like officers).
- **HER-013** (`decided`): A Card MAY let the player choose a hero to join a Brigade (e.g. "Choose a hero to join your Brigade").
- **HER-014** (`decided`): Heroes MUST progress through veterancy, the same way as officers (see [[officer-veterancy]]).
- **HER-015** (`decided`): A hero MUST NOT take up a Section slot; it is an extra on top of the Brigade's 3–5 Sections. Heroes are meant to be rare, so a player has maybe one or two extra-strong Brigades.
- **HER-016** (`decided`): A Brigade MUST always have an officer. A hero is an addition, never a replacement.
- **HER-017** (`decided`): In multiplayer, all heroes MUST be available, but each player picks a roster of 5 heroes before the match (P-HER-03) and may only use those.
- **HER-020** (`decided`): A hero MUST join a Brigade directly through a Card, **immediately** and wherever that Brigade is, so they can join the fighting straight away. The Brigade must not already have a hero (HER-009).
- **HER-021** (`decided`): A hero gained from a campaign story event MUST join a Brigade immediately. If no Brigade can take a hero (every Brigade already has one), the player MUST choose to either replace an existing hero (who goes to the collection) or send the new hero to the collection.
- **HER-006** (`decided`): A Faction MUST have at most 3 heroes (P-HER-02).
- **HER-018** (`decided`): The CP cost of hero Cards MUST go up with each hero acquired (P-HER-04).
- **HER-019** (`decided`): The campaign MUST include set story events that give the player a hero.
- **HER-007** (`decided`): Heroes MAY be real historical figures or fictional characters (SET-005, SET-006).
