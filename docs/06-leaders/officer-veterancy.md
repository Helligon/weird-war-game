# Veterancy model (officers, heroes, Sections)

## Summary
A mix: Sections, officers and heroes each gain veterancy in their own way. Officer veterancy gives plain bonuses to the Sections the officer leads. Section veterancy stays with the Section. Hero veterancy improves the hero. All veterancy bonuses are capped.

## Three progression layers
| Layer | Belongs to | Applies to | Nature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section veterancy ([[veterancy]]) | The Section (travels with it between Brigades) | That Section | Small random stat traits |
| Officer veterancy | The officer (stays with them) | Sections currently under their command | Basic, standard bonuses |
| Hero veterancy | The hero | The hero's own stats | Basic stat bonuses |
| *Officer bonuses ([[officers]])* | *The officer* | *Brigade / battle / map* | *Flavourful, named, max 3; not veterancy* |

## Rules
- **OVT-001** (`decided`): Officers MUST gain veterancy by surviving battles.
- **OVT-002** (`decided`): Officer veterancy bonuses MUST apply to the Sections currently under that officer's command. A Section that leaves the Brigade MUST lose them, and a Section that joins MUST gain them.
- **OVT-003** (`decided`): Officer veterancy bonuses MUST stay basic and standard (e.g. "+5% health for Sections under this officer's command"). Flavour belongs to officer bonuses (OFF-007).
- **OVT-004** (`decided`): This MUST apply to officers who were AROs as well as to collection officers.
- **OVT-005** (`decided`): Heroes MUST gain veterancy that improves their own stats (HER-014).
- **OVT-006** (`decided`): Section veterancy MUST stay with the Section when it moves between Brigades (VET-003).
- **OVT-007** (`decided`): The total bonus from veterancy MUST be capped at +20% (P-OVT-01).
- **OVT-008** (`decided`): The cap MUST apply **per stat, per Unit, across all veterancy sources combined** (Section veterancy + officer veterancy, or hero veterancy). Officer bonuses and ARO "lesson learned" bonuses are not veterancy and are not capped by it.
