# Decision log

Significant design decisions, recorded in a lightweight version of the ADR (Architecture Decision Record) format. Newest decisions are added at the bottom. Decisions are never edited; a later decision replaces an earlier one and links back to it.

| ID | Date | Decision | Reasoning | Affects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-001 | 2026-11-19 | Two layers: a turn-based campaign plus real-time RTS/third-person battles. | Core concept. | [[vision]] |
| DEC-002 | 2026-11-19 | Armies are called **Brigades**. | Fits the WW1 setting. | [[brigades]] |
| DEC-003 | 2026-11-19 | Supply is measured against each Brigade's capacity, with 4 states. | Bigger Brigades need more supply; readable UI. | [[supply]] |
| DEC-004 | 2026-11-19 | "Middle" supply-line model: resupply only at depots or FOBs, or by Card. | Makes depots and flanking matter without heavy bookkeeping. | [[supply]] |

| DEC-007 | 2026-11-19 | One HQ (lose it = lose the game), several FOBs, capturable depots. | Clear objectives and stakes. | [[map-and-locations]] |
| DEC-008 | 2026-11-19 | A captured FOB becomes a neutral supply depot. | The captor gets the reward; the loser can't simply retake it. | [[map-and-locations]] |
| DEC-009 | 2026-11-19 | Overextended deployment uses preset patterns, weighted by terrain. | Flavourful, and readable but not fully predictable. | [[deployment]] |
| DEC-010 | 2026-11-19 | Similar-Section Brigades get class bonuses. | Encourages specialised Brigades. | [[brigades]] |
| DEC-011 | 2026-11-19 | Three Factions: Central Powers, Eastern Allies, Western Allies. Standard roster plus faction Sections. | Asymmetry on a shared core. | [[factions-overview]] |
| DEC-012 | 2026-11-19 | New Brigades are bought Section by Section with SP at FOBs. | Player agency. | [[brigades]] |

| DEC-016 | 2026-11-20 | The player plays as an officer or hero and gives orders from a tactical view. | The RTS/third-person hybrid. | [[player-character]], [[commands]] |

| DEC-023 | 2026-11-21 | Sections in defences hold by default; otherwise they engage and take cover. | Defences make sense; players are encouraged to give orders. | [[default-ai-behaviour]] |

| DEC-026 | 2026-11-21 | Brigades only ever spend SP from their own supply; upkeep never comes directly from the reserve. | Keeps logistics local; the reserve only reaches Brigades through resupply. | [[upkeep-and-desertion]], [[supply]] |
| DEC-027 | 2026-11-21 | Iron Officers: dead officers are gone for the rest of the game and an ARO loses their progress. Standard mode: all officers, AROs included, are wounded and recover. | One clear rule per mode. | [[officers]], [[aro-progression]] |

| DEC-029 | 2026-11-21 | Morale model: Brigade base %, each Section starts a battle at the base, chance to rout = 100 − 5 × morale below 20%. | A simple, readable curve. | [[morale]] |
| DEC-030 | 2026-11-21 | Campaign map is a hex grid. Brigade capacity = 10 hexes + 1 battle. | Gives the supply budget a concrete anchor. | [[supply]], [[map-and-locations]] |
| DEC-031 | 2026-11-21 | Resupply is free; it is limited only by being on or next to a depot or FOB. | Keeps logistics simple. | [[supply]] |
| DEC-032 | 2026-11-21 | Single-Section orders only (≤10 Sections per side). | Group selection isn't needed at this scale. | [[commands]] |
| DEC-033 | 2026-11-21 | Auto-resolve formula deferred to a working session. | Needs real values and example Brigades first. | [[battle-initiation]] |
| DEC-034 | 2026-11-21 | Campaign turns alternate; battles start immediately on engagement. | Simpler for the AI and for 2-player. | [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-035 | 2026-11-21 | An ST is captured by a Brigade staying on it for a turn. | Holding ground means something. | [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-036 | 2026-11-21 | Rout chance is rolled only on morale-changing events; the formula stays tunable. | Readable and predictable. | [[morale]] |
| DEC-037 | 2026-11-21 | Supply scales with Brigade size (capacity and cost per hex). Section types can carry supply modifiers; undead use less supply but have a movement limit. | Flavour plus a trade-off between size and reach. | [[supply]], [[unit-tags]] |
| DEC-038 | 2026-11-21 | No general movement cap; a **Slow** tag caps movement for some Sections (including undead). | SP stays the main limit; Slow adds flavour. | [[supply]], [[unit-tags]] |
| DEC-039 | 2026-11-21 | Battles on ST hexes: the winner holds or takes the hex; a successful defender's capture carries on. | Clear rules for contested captures. | [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-040 | 2026-11-21 | After a battle: defenders retreat towards the nearest HQ/FOB; attackers fall back to the hex they came from; a Brigade that can't retreat is destroyed; an ST gives no income while an enemy sits on it. | Clear results on the map. | [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-041 | 2026-11-21 | Strange Sections spend SP; AP is for Cards only. Replaces the earlier AP-upkeep proposal. | Keeps the two currencies distinct. | [[unit-tags]] |
| DEC-042 | 2026-11-21 | Undead rot (take losses) unless they fought that turn. Thralls/Ghouls are Slow; Vampires are not. | Flavour: undead must keep fighting. | [[supply]] |
| DEC-043 | 2026-11-21 | A Brigade moves at the speed of its slowest Section. | Simple and intuitive. | [[supply]] |
| DEC-044 | 2026-11-21 | A retreat is 1 free hex, only towards the nearest friendly HQ/FOB; a Brigade with no such hex is destroyed. | Being cut off from your bases is deadly. | [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-045 | 2026-11-21 | Undead get a 2-turn grace period before rotting. | Lets them reposition; tunable. | [[supply]] |
| DEC-046 | 2026-11-21 | One Brigade per hex; a retreating Brigade passes through a friendly one by moving 1 extra hex. Impassable hexes: terrain, the map edge, enemy Brigades. | Clear rules for space on the map. | [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-047 | 2026-11-21 | Mountains, sea and rivers are impassable for ground Brigades (marsh maybe); air vehicles fly over all terrain. | Terrain shapes the front; air units bypass it. | [[turn-structure]], [[vehicles]] |
| DEC-048 | 2026-11-21 | Two combat types: standard battle and one-sided bombardment (artillery, HQ, FOB from range; bombers adjacent). Enemy Brigades block air movement except when attacked. | Ranged support and air power without full battles. | [[bombardment]], [[turn-structure]] |
| DEC-049 | 2026-11-21 | Air and Sea Brigades are designed and built after ground units. | Manages scope; ground is the core. | [[vehicles]], [[AGENTS]] |
| DEC-050 | 2026-11-22 | No Captain tier. Heroes have special weapons and gear, max 1 per Brigade, come from Cards, story events and achievements, and grow through veterancy like officers. | Keeps leader types simple. | [[heroes]] |

| DEC-052 | 2026-11-22 | Hero cap of 3 per Faction, with rising Card costs and set story events. No extra ARO limit. A wiped-out Brigade's officer returns to the roster after 3 turns. | DEC-053 | 2026-11-22 | Wounded officer → acting officer from the roster; on recovery the player picks who leads. Capacity overflow is kept until spent. | DEC-054 | 2026-11-22 | Wounds last 3 turns. Generic Officer or ARO as the fallback. A hard cap of 5 Sections, which a Card or officer bonus can raise by 1. An empty Brigade is disbanded. | Closes the gaps in the Brigade lifecycle. | [[officers]], [[brigades]] |
| DEC-055 | 2026-11-22 | Cards: deck and hand (start with 5, draw 1 per turn, max 8), campaign map only, no play limits or cooldowns, shared core plus faction Cards, play conditions, unlocked through the campaign, multiplayer deck building. | Card-game feel; rarity through copies and AP. | [[cards]] |
