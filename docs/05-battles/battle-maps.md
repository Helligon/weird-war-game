# Battle maps

## Summary
Battle maps are generated from the terrain of the campaign location where the battle takes place.

## Rules
- **BMP-001** (`decided`): A battle map MUST be generated from the campaign location's terrain.
- **BMP-002** (`decided`): A battle at an ST MUST give the defender defences, as ST-specific cover on the battlefield (BMP-008).
- **BMP-007** (`decided`): The defended ST itself MUST sit just off the battle map: visible as a backdrop but not part of the battlefield.
- **BMP-008** (`decided`): ST-specific cover MUST be placed in front of the ST on the defender's side, for example:

| Location | Cover |
|---|---|
| HQ / FOB | Trenches (HQ: full fortifications) |
| Major ST | Trench networks and bunkers |
| Supply / Command ST | Sandbags and barbed wire |
| Specific STs | Themed cover, e.g. log barricades at a factory |

- **BMP-009** (`decided`): Battle maps MUST be built from hand-made map pieces for each terrain type (e.g. village, forest, trench line, river crossing), chosen to match the battle hex.
- **BMP-010** (`decided`): Battle maps MUST be small enough that infantry can cross them in about 60–90 seconds (P-BMP-01).
- **BMP-003** (`decided`): Battle-map terrain MUST weight the selection of overextended deployment patterns (see [[deployment]]).
- **BMP-004** (`decided`): Each side MUST have its own map edge, which routing Units flee towards (see [[victory-and-routing]]).
- **BMP-005** (`decided`): Battle maps MUST include cover that Sections can use (see [[commands]]).
- **BMP-006** (`decided`): An HQ battle MUST include the HQ's fortifications and garrison (see [[map-and-locations]]).
