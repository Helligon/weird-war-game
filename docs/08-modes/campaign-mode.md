# Campaign mode

## Summary
A single-player campaign against AI Factions, similar to other strategy games.

## Rules
- **CMP-001** (`decided`): The campaign MUST be single-player against AI opponents.
- **CMP-002** (`decided`): Auto-resolve MUST always be available in the campaign (BIN-003).
- **CMP-003** (`decided`): The campaign MUST offer the Iron Officers setting (OFF-009).
- **CMP-004** (`proposed`): Iron Officers SHOULD be chosen when the campaign starts, as in XCOM's Ironman mode.
- **CMP-006** (`decided`): The campaign MUST be a series of hand-made maps telling a story. Each map is a conquest scenario with set story events (e.g. gaining heroes, freeing imprisoned officers).
- **CMP-007** (`decided`): At points the player MUST choose their next mission, each with different trade-offs (e.g. battle 1 rewards an Engineer Section, battle 2 a Sniper Section).
- **CMP-008** (`decided`): Destroying the enemy HQ MUST always win a map. Maps MAY also have their own objectives (e.g. hold 3 Major STs for 5 turns, survive 20 turns).
- **CMP-009** (`decided`): There MUST be special scenarios, e.g. **Onslaught**: waves of enemy Brigades attack and the player builds defences and holds out for X turns.
- **CMP-010** (`decided`): The player MUST be able to save at any time on the campaign map, but not mid-battle, in both normal and Iron Officers modes.
- **CMP-011** (`decided`): In Iron Officers mode, there MUST be a single save slot that is always overwritten, so a dead officer can't be undone by loading an older save.
- **CMP-012** (`decided`): The Iron Officers option MUST clearly explain, when ticked, that it means permadeath **and** a single overwritten save.
- **CMP-013** (`decided`): In Onslaught and similar scenarios, defences MUST come from Cards (e.g. Dig in, Fortified Position, Hasty Repairs), capturing STs, upgrading STs so they can defend themselves, and fielding Brigades that counter the incoming enemy.
- **CMP-005** (`decided`): Pausing in the tactical view MUST be enabled (CMD-002).

## Open questions
- Q-CMP-01 (answered → CMP-006, CMP-007): A story told through hand-made maps, with branching mission choices.
- Q-CMP-02 (answered → CMP-008, CMP-009): The HQ always wins; per-map objectives; Onslaught scenarios.
- Q-CMP-04 (answered → CMP-013): Cards, capturing STs, upgrading STs, counter-Brigades.
- Q-CMP-06 (answered → MAP-046 to MAP-049): The only upgrade is to an FOB; Garrison Sections can be placed on STs.
- Q-CMP-03 (answered → CMP-010): Save any time on the map, in both modes.
- Q-CMP-05 (answered → CMP-011, CMP-012): Yes, explained when the box is ticked.
