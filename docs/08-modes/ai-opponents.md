# AI opponents

## Summary
AI is needed on the campaign map (strategic) and in battle (tactical) for the campaign and for bot matches.

## Rules
- **AIO-001** (`decided`): The campaign MUST have a strategic AI controlling the opposing Factions.
- **AIO-002** (`decided`): Bot opponents MUST have adjustable difficulty.
- **AIO-003** (`decided`): A battle AI MUST command enemy Sections in real-time battles.
- **AIO-006** (`decided`): Difficulty MUST change both how well the AI decides and, on Hard and above, its bonus income.
- **AIO-007** (`decided`): The battle AI MUST use the same commands and limits as the player.
- **AIO-004** (`proposed`): Campaign rules SHOULD stay clear and deterministic (supply states, fixed costs) to keep the strategic AI tractable.
- **AIO-005** (`proposed`): AI-vs-AI battles SHOULD be auto-resolved (BIN-007).
