# Glossary

Single source of truth for terms. Terms marked *(proposed)* are not yet confirmed.

## Organisation
### Faction
A playable side made up of several nations. See [[factions-overview]].
### Unit
A single fighting member (one soldier, one landship, one creature). See [[hierarchy]].
### Section
A group of Units. Its size is driven by its health and can be as small as 1 Unit. See [[hierarchy]].
### Brigade
A group of 3–5 Sections led by an Officer. The campaign-map army piece. See [[brigades]].
### Officer
A separate Unit that leads a Brigade and carries bonuses. See [[officers]].
### Generic Officer
A fallback officer with basic stats and no bonuses.
### Acting Ranking Officer (ARO)
A Unit promoted to officer who earns bonuses over 5 battles. See [[aro-progression]].
### Hero
A named character with special weapons and gear. Max 1 per Brigade. See [[heroes]].

## Resources
### Supply Points (SP)
Logistics currency: pays for movement, upkeep and forming Brigades. See [[resources]].
### Command Points (CP)
Command currency: spent on Cards. See [[resources]].
### Reserve
The Faction's central pool of SP.
### Supply capacity
The maximum SP a Brigade can carry (its supply train).
### Supply state
Well Supplied / Supplied / Low / Overextended. Derived from a Brigade's SP relative to its capacity. See [[supply]].
### Overextended
A Brigade that attacked or defended in a battle with SP below the Overextended threshold (P-SUP-13).
### Upkeep
The SP cost per turn of keeping a Brigade in the field. See [[upkeep-and-desertion]].
### Desertion
Health loss caused by unpaid upkeep.

## Map
### Hex
One space on the campaign map's hex grid.
### Strategic Target (ST)
A capturable map location that gives SP and/or CP bonuses.
### Headquarters (HQ)
The Faction's single fortified base. Losing it loses the game.
### Forward Operating Base (FOB)
A resupply point that generates SP and where new Brigades can be formed.
### Supply ST
An ST that generates SP and is a resupply point (on or adjacent). Replaces the retired term "supply depot".
### Territory perimeter
The boundary around a Faction's captured points, plus padding. Upkeep is higher outside it.
### Field depot
A Supply ST placed by the single-use Field depot Card.
### Garrison Section
A basic, special Section placed on an owned ST to slow attackers. It fights auto-resolved battles and costs no upkeep.
### Heavy weaponry
Any Section with the Heavy or Artillery class tag. Needed to properly damage HQs and FOBs.
### Levelled FOB
An FOB reduced to 0 health (by bombardment or assault). It becomes a neutral Supply ST.

## Battle
### Tactical view
The command view where Sections are given orders.
### Waypoint
A movement target in a Move order (max 2).
### Morale
A percentage. Brigades have a base morale; Sections have their own morale in battle. Low morale causes routing. See [[morale]].
### Rout
A Unit fleeing towards its own map edge.
### Deployment pattern
A preset arrangement forced on Overextended Brigades.
### Reinforcement
A second Brigade joining a battle partway through.
### Bombardment
One-sided ranged combat (artillery, HQ, bombers; not FOBs) where the target can't fire back. See [[bombardment]].
### Auto-resolve
Resolving a battle by calculation instead of playing it.
### Retreat
*(proposed)* Leaving a battle before it starts by spending SP.

## Progression
### Veterancy
Random small stat bonuses gained by surviving Sections.
### Officer veterancy
*(proposed)* Basic Brigade-wide bonuses gained by an officer.
### Officer bonus
A flavourful named bonus held by an officer (max 3 slots).
### Lesson learned
A small stacking stat bonus an ARO gains from a loss.
### Iron Officers
A campaign setting where officers and heroes die permanently.
### Wounded
An officer out of action for some turns (when Iron Officers is off).

## Unit identity
### Class tag
Infantry / Heavy / Artillery / Support. See [[unit-tags]].
### Origin tag
*(proposed)* Mundane / Strange.
### Strange Brigade
A Brigade made up mostly or entirely of creatures and fantastical beings.
### Seconded unit
A Section lent by an allied Entente faction.
### Defector
A Section that has switched sides.
### Thrall / Ghoul
Infantry turned by a vampire or dark magic.

## Cards
### Card
A one-off effect played on the campaign map by paying CP. Drawn from a deck into a hand (max 8). See [[cards]].
### Hand
The Cards a player currently holds (max 8).
### Fog of war
Hides STs and enemy Brigades outside sight; terrain is always visible. See [[fog-of-war]].
### Sight
How many hexes a Section can see (1–3).
