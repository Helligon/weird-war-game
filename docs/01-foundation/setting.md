# Setting

## Summary
An alternate history of the First World War with steampunk and science-fiction weaponry and fantastical creatures.

## Rules
- **SET-001** (`decided`): The setting MUST be an alternate-history WW1.
- **SET-002** (`decided`): Weaponry MUST include steampunk / science-fiction technology.
- **SET-003** (`decided`): The world MAY include fantastical creatures and beings (see [[unit-tags#Origin tag]]).
- **SET-004** (`decided`): Terms SHOULD loosely follow WW1 military naming (e.g. Section, Brigade, Forward Operating Base (FOB)).
- **SET-005** (`decided`, DEC-078): Named characters and Brigades MAY use **real historical names** (e.g. Haig, the Red Baron). Real figures MUST NOT be portrayed as particularly bad; they are simply on different sides.
- **SET-006** (`decided`): Fictional characters MAY be added alongside real ones, especially for monster-hunting roles (e.g. Van Helsing).

## Interactions
- [[factions-overview]]: factions are built on real WW1 alliances with fantastical flavours.

## Open questions
- Q-SET-02 (answered → SET-005, SET-006): Real names, with fictional monster hunters alongside.
- Q-SET-01: Final game title (*Weird War* is a working title from the repo name).
