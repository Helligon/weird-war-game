# Vehicles

> [!warning] Stub
> Vehicles have **not been discussed yet**.

> [!important] Sequencing (decided)
> **Air and Sea Brigades MUST be designed and completed after ground units are finished.** Air and sea rules below are captured but deferred.

## Known so far
- **VEH-001** (`decided`): A large vehicle such as a landship MUST be a Section of a single Unit (HIE-003).
- **VEH-002** (`decided`): Heavy infantry are mechanised (ROS-001).
- **VEH-003** (`decided`): Britain has science-fiction / steampunk mechanised Sections (WAL-002).
- **VEH-004** (`decided`): Only heavy weaponry can damage an HQ (MAP-005).
- **VEH-006** (`decided`): Air vehicles (e.g. zeppelins, biplanes) MUST be able to fly over all terrain types.
- **VEH-007** (`deferred`): Gunships able to travel across water hexes.
- **VEH-008** (`deferred`): **Air Brigades** (Brigades made of air Sections) and **Sea Brigades** MUST exist eventually.
- **VEH-010** (`deferred`): Air bomber units are organised as a **Bomber Squadron** (working term).
- **VEH-009** (`deferred`): Bombers bomb adjacent hexes; fighters attack air Brigades (see [[bombardment]]).
- **VEH-005** (`proposed`): A light armoured car SHOULD be part of the standard roster.
